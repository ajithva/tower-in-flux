Tower in Flux

The tower here isn't designed. It grows from simple local rules: each new block appears or doesn't depending on how many neighbours it has on the level below. Blocks with nothing under them fall, pile up as rubble, and can become ground for more building. Once the tower settles, the wind starts, and it gets stronger in random gusts.

Your goal: get the tower up to the target height, then keep it standing for as long as you can.

1

Tune the rules. Choose the grid size, seed, density and growth rules. They decide how solid or lacy the tower comes out.

2

Press New game. The tower grows level by level, then anything unsupported falls and lands as rubble.

3

Keep building. While the wind is blowing, press Continue growing to add more levels on top, until you pass the target.

4

Score with each move. A build scores when the tower settles: 100 points for each new level above your best. Reaching the target earns a bonus, and a bigger one if you get there in fewer moves. Gusts can knock height off, and height you rebuild scores nothing.

How Tower in Flux works

The idea

Tower in Flux is a 3D cellular automaton that started as a Grasshopper script. Instead of drawing a tower, you set a few local rules and watch a form grow out of them. Then gravity and wind test how well it holds together.

How a level grows

Level 0 is scattered randomly (controlled by Density and Seed), and the centre block is always filled. Each new level looks at the 8 cells around each position on the level below, counting both tower blocks and rubble:

neighbours between Birth min and Birth max → block is born
block directly below and neighbours ≥ Survive min → block survives
otherwise → empty

With Pure birth on, only the birth rule applies, which gives sparser, more fragile towers.

The three phases

  • Growing: the tower is built one level at a time.
  • Collapsing: any block with nothing under it falls, unless it's held on both sides. Falling blocks drift outward and land as rubble, which piles a few blocks high.
  • Wind: the upper part of the tower (from 70% of the level count up) sways. Blocks with fewer neighbours sway more, and if one sways too far it breaks off and falls.

Gusts

While the wind blows, it picks up in random gusts every few seconds, each adding 10–40% more strength, up to ×4. Tall, thin and exposed blocks go first. Only New game or Reset brings the wind back down.

Scoring

Only your moves score. A move is New game (your first build) or Continue growing. Each one is scored once the tower has finished collapsing and settled.

  • +100 per new level: you only score height above the best height you've reached so far. If the wind knocked levels off, rebuilding them scores nothing.
  • Target bonus: the first move that settles at or above the target height earns target × 100.
  • Par bonus: par is the number of builds you'd expect to need (target ÷ levels per build, rounded up, + 1). Each move under par adds 1,000.
  • The wind never gives points. It only takes height away, which makes your next move harder.
  • Your best score is saved in this browser.

Tips

  • Wide, dense footprints (a high Density with Birth min around 3) stand up to wind much better than thin ones.
  • Continue growing only works during the Wind phase. It reseeds the chosen level and adds Levels more on top.
  • With Build on rubble on, the tower can spread onto the rubble around its base and brace itself.
  • Clear rubble takes away that support, and anything resting on it falls.

Lighting

Under Lighting & display, choose Day, Night or Cycle. In Cycle the sun crosses the sky, the moon and stars come out, and after dusk random cubes in the tower light up like windows and switch on and off. Day length sets how long a full day takes, and Glow turns the bloom effect on or off. The lighting is purely visual and doesn't change the simulation.

Controls

Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, and right-drag to pan. The Speed slider changes how fast the simulation runs.
